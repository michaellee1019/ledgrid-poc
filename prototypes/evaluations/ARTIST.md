# Installation artist / show-author evaluation

## Evaluator and method

I approached these as a technically literate installation artist who understands layers, previews, cues, masks, and rehearsals, but has never seen this product. My common task is: identify what the room is doing, find or compose a look without disturbing it, make the performance boundary explicit, and recover confidently when something is unavailable or another operator has changed the wall.

I used every candidate cold in the browser at desktop width and again at approximately 390 px before reading any candidate documentation. I treated fixture actions as simulations and recorded the point where the interface made me believe the physical wall *would* change. I then read the candidate notes plus the repository's web and animation-system contracts for backend-fit scoring. I did not modify any candidate.

Scores are 1–5. The weighted total is `sum(score / 5 × category weight)`.

The gap is not a taste judgment. Wall Conductor won because it kept identity, preview provenance, state boundaries, and the physical consequence legible through every adventure. Lumen Path is the strongest activity-led alternative and the best phone product, but its moment editor produced a stale preview identity. Room Tune is the easiest household appliance, but its scene authoring and backend mapping are less exact. Finder is information-dense and operationally credible until it offers a normal take-live confirmation for build-only content. Lumen Loom has the best spatial comparison idea, but hidden preset identity, a missing native fallback, misleading availability actions, and a nonfunctional phone posture make it unsafe as the primary control surface.

## 1. Wall Conductor — 98/100

### Verdict

This is the most trustworthy show-authoring tool. Its sequence—Now, Find, Audition, Compare, Compose, Take live—matches how I build confidence. It makes the 32 × 138 shape useful instead of decorative, keeps provider-qualified identity attached to choices, and gives the physical wall a persistent truth strip. I would use this during a public installation without first needing to learn its internal taxonomy.

### Adventure log

1. **Cold orientation and safe stop**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I expected the persistent live-colored strip to be physical truth. `Stop wall` was my first click because it sat beside `Live · Nocturne Ferns / Cozy Canopy · saved preset`, power, brightness, and Vibe.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Live strip → `Stop wall`. No wrong turn. It immediately changed to `Wall stopped` and explained that output was black while power remained on.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Full live component and preset identity, saved state, power, brightness, and global Vibe were visible without hover. I believed the wall would change at the initial Stop click, not while browsing.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the persistent physical-truth strip. Let the stopped state retain the last live identity as `held/last output` so a restart is equally predictable.

2. **Find calm among 292, audition, compare, perform**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I wanted an intent-led entry rather than guessing a component name. From Now, `Settle the room` looked like the right first choice.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Now → `Settle the room` → Find, prefilled `quiet` → `75 of 292` → switchable `292 Presets / 52 Components` → select `001 Nocturne Ferns` → `Add to compare` → add `002 Tidepool` and `003 After-Rain Clock` → `Review 3 side by side` → choose a candidate → `Take this preset live` → confirm. No recovery was needed.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The reading index carried complete names, descriptions, component, provider, role, and readiness. The comparison showed three actual tall previews with provider-qualified IDs; it did not collapse them into thumbnails. Preview copy said it was isolated. I believed the wall would change only on the confirmation's final take-live action.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the textual index feeding one large audition stage and a bounded three-up comparison. On phone, shrink the persistent compare tray so it does not cover the list and audition.

3. **Receiver-native provenance and unavailable content**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I expected a native item to be previewable but not necessarily runnable. I selected a receiver-native result to inspect the evidence.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Find → receiver-native `Nocturne Ferns` → inspect preview caption and provider-qualified ID → select `Plant Calibration Sweep` marked `Catalog-build only`.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The native audition said `Host-built simulation · not receiver framebuffer readback or exact live output`; the build-only item remained visible and previewable, while its performance action became the disabled `Visible, but not executable here`. Provider, role, and availability never depended on hover. No physical change was implied anywhere in this path.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the three separate facts—provider, preview provenance, performability. Add the concrete build or deployment prerequisite beside `not executable here` for expert recovery.

4. **Compose, provoke draft/drift, validate, save only, take live**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Compose` looked like a bounded score editor. I expected one background and one clock, not an unlimited layer stack.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Compose → background `Nocturne Ferns` (host Python, known fallback `Safe Night`) + `After-Rain Clock` → change opacity `82% → 65%` and vertical translation to `8 LEDs` → read `Unsaved draft · live output untouched` and `Preset source drift` → `Validate` → `Save layout only` → verify live strip remained stopped → `Take validated scene live` → review `Take this look live?` → `Take live now`.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Background and overlay names, providers, roles, fallback, clip policy, and stale policy (`30s`, hide, stop) stayed attached to their tracks. The whole-scene canvas was labeled isolated. Save explicitly excluded Vibe and plants. I believed the physical wall would change only on `Take live now` inside the second-step dialog.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.** One friction: before revalidation, the take-live entry still looked enabled while drift was present; validation and the confirmation prevented ambiguity, but the entry affordance should reflect the gate.
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve Validate, Save layout only, and Take live as three separate verbs. Disable or relabel take-live whenever the current revision is not the validated revision.

5. **Change Vibe and plant material independently**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I expected room-wide treatment in a global surface, not inside the scene inspector. `Room layers` in the live strip looked correct.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** `Room layers` → select `Vivid` → foliage strength `70 → 50` → observe `Room changes not yet applied` → `Apply room layers`.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The drawer repeatedly said the controls were independent of content and not scene content. The scene name did not change. I believed globals would change only on `Apply room layers`.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the independent drawer and explicit apply. Summarize active exclusive field/surface plant semantics in the persistent strip after application.

6. **Expected-degraded receiver diagnosis**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I expected a plain answer to “can the show continue?” before raw metrics. `Health` was the obvious destination.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Health → headline `wall keeping up` → four receivers → Receiver 3 → expand expected limited-return evidence.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Receiver 3 said it was active with incomplete telemetry and unreported release acceptance, then named the `Expected degraded policy`; overall output remained operational. It did not equate missing return data with failed playback. This path had no wall-changing action.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the conclusion-first evidence ladder. Add a timestamp and source revision to distinguish fresh agreement from cached status.

7. **Rehearse point and hole input**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I expected direct interaction to live under a dedicated performance surface. `Touch` looked safer than tapping an audition.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Touch → choose `Placed hole` → tap the tall wall → coordinate report `x15, y72`; inspect Primary point, Random hole, Placed hole, strength, and game controls.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The interaction surface used the exact wall shape and logical `32 × 138` coordinates. Live/preview targeting was named. I believed a live gesture would be sent when tapping the live-labeled canvas after choosing a mode.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 4/5.** The immediate tap is appropriate for play, but a conspicuous live-target accent should persist under the finger.
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the dedicated takeover surface and coordinate feedback. Add an optional `stage then send` mode for destructive or large-radius gestures.

8. **Phone authoring at ~390 px**
   - **Starting assumption / first tap:** I expected the same `Settle the room` task with live truth still reachable. The persistent top strip and bottom navigation made that first tap obvious.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Now → `Settle the room` → Find → scan wrapped names → select exact tall preview → build comparison. The compare tray then occupied much of the viewport and obscured the work beneath it; filter controls also required horizontal travel.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Identity, provider, and preview/live labels remained complete, and the final confirmation boundary remained available. I did not cross it in the phone repeat; the visible confirmation was the expected physical-change moment.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Partial, 4/5.** The task was finishable but crowded once comparison was populated.
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the top live truth and bottom place navigation. Collapse the compare set to a one-line count until opened, and give filters a deliberate scroll affordance.

### Why these scores

The candidate earns 5s for orientation, preview safety, catalog, scene/global model, recovery/provenance, backend fit, and accessibility because the running UI demonstrated each contract rather than only documenting it. Its API map cleanly separates status/stop, unified provider-qualified catalog, isolated preview, scene validation/preview/presets, Vibe, plant modifiers, interactions, and receiver refresh. Keyboard `/`, arrows, and compare shortcut complement complete wrapping. Phone is a 4 because the expanded compare tray materially obstructs authoring.

## 2. Lumen Path (cold journey) — 92/100

### Verdict

Lumen Path has the best answer to “why is the wall on?” A timed journey with turns is a powerful show-authoring object, and the private-rehearsal grammar is exceptionally safe. It is also the best phone candidate. I rank it below Wall Conductor because expert scene editing can silently desynchronize the selected material and the preview label, which damages the very rehearsal trust the product depends on.

### Adventure log

1. **Cold orientation and safe stop**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** The header said `LIVE NOW · Deep Current + North Star · Matches saved`, but I expected Stop near it. My first click was the live ribbon itself because no Stop was visible.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** `LIVE NOW` → Live controls drawer → `Stop scene` → header `Scene stopped · wall idle`. This was one extra discovery click, not a dead end.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Full scene identity and saved relation were visible. Brightness, power, FPS, motion, and Stop were correctly scoped as live controls. I believed the physical wall would change on `Stop scene`.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 4/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the compact live ribbon and drawer detail. Keep an exposed emergency Stop on desktop and phone.

2. **Find calm, rehearse, compare, perform**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Calm the room` was a natural first action for a show author thinking in room states rather than assets.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Trailhead → `Calm the room` → 45-minute `Exhale → Gather inward → Land softly` journey → scrub private rehearsal → Field guide → search/filter across `292 of 292` → add three to comparison → compare tall rehearsals → review/take live. No content taxonomy was required for the initial success; the full guide remained available when I wanted ingredients.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Journey and turn names led, while field-guide cards and comparison retained full preset, component, provider, role, readiness, and preview truth. The perform review contrasted on-wall and proposed states, required wall acknowledgement, and then a one-second hold. I believed the wall would change only when the hold completed.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve purpose → journey → turn → rehearsal as a first-class authoring flow, backed by a complete secondary field guide. Let comparison candidates share a scrub time for motion comparison.

3. **Receiver-native provenance and unavailable content**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** In the Field guide I expected readiness to constrain action without hiding the item.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Field guide → receiver-native card/compare → read `host simulation · not framebuffer readback` → search `Plant Mask Preview Tidepool` → open its unavailable rehearsal.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Cards and compare kept provider and role visible. The unavailable item could be rehearsed and compared, but the action in its rehearsal was a disabled `Unavailable`. No live mutation was suggested.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve discoverable, rehearsal-only unavailable entries. Explain the remediation (`build`, `deploy`, `policy`, or `quarantine review`) beside readiness.

4. **Compose, save-only, and take live**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Tune this moment` suggested editing the selected journey turn without altering the live wall.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Journey → `Tune this moment` → Scene Studio → `Change` background → unexpectedly cycles directly to receiver-native `Compiled Rainbow` rather than opening a chooser → adjust clock opacity `82%`, placement `y -12`, and stale behavior `hold / clear after 30s` → verify known Python fallback `Ember Moss` → Validate → `Save layout only` → review/take live → acknowledge intended wall → hold.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The scene contract and fallback were excellent, and save left the wall idle with `Differs from saved`. The serious wrong turn was identity drift inside the editor: after the background changed and validated, the isolated preview heading still said `Breathing Moss + clock`. I could not trust whether the image, heading, or staged receipt was authoritative. The wall-change moment was still unambiguous: completion of the final hold.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Partial, 3/5** for authoring despite a safe apply boundary.
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the on-wall-versus-proposed receipt and hold. Make `Change` open an explicit chooser, and update preview image, heading, provider, validation revision, and receipt atomically.

5. **Change Vibe and plant material independently**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Vibe & plants` appeared as a global control outside Scene Studio, which matched my mental model.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Open `Vibe & plants` → select `Vivid` → try `Illuminate` and `Portal` → summary remains `shadow / obstacle` → Apply global settings.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The dialog explicitly said atmosphere is independent of scenes and journeys, and applying it did not rename the scene. Vibe feedback worked; plant selection feedback appeared inert, so I could not tell whether the clicks were accepted. I believed globals would change on Apply, not while rehearsing.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Partial, 3/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the independent atmosphere dialog. Make each plant selection visibly update its resolved sentence and block Apply if UI and state disagree.

6. **Expected-degraded receiver diagnosis**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I expected Wall care to answer whether the planned activity could continue.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Wall care → readiness summary → receiver detail → expand one-way telemetry explanation.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** All strips showed frame flow; the two write-only receivers were labeled expected one-way/expected degraded, with no claim of acknowledgement or release acceptance. This was honest about usable playback agreement and its limits. No physical change existed on this path.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve activity readiness followed by transport evidence. Add the observed receipt/revision and age of evidence.

7. **Rehearse point and hole input**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Interact with moment` suggested rehearsal rather than an immediate live injection.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Interact with moment → gesture sandbox → choose `Punch hole` → center → `Review one live gesture` → inspect `Confirm send punch hole x16, y69`.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The interface separated rehearsal from send and exposed exact coordinates. I believed the wall would change only on `Confirm send`, not on the sandbox tap.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the stage-then-confirm gesture grammar. Add radius/strength to the final receipt whenever those values are non-default.

8. **Phone authoring at ~390 px**
   - **Starting assumption / first tap:** I expected the same Calm journey to lead. Purpose cards were a thumb-friendly single trail below a compact live ribbon.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Home → `Calm the room` → rehearsal before turn list → save-only/take-live actions → contextual `Tune this moment` when deeper composition was needed. Bottom navigation stayed focused on Home, Journey, Explore, and Care.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Full purpose, journey, and turn names wrapped; the private rehearsal retained the exact tall shape and isolated label. The perform review remained a bottom sheet with an explicit hold boundary. No wrong turn.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve contextual Scene Studio rather than crowding it into the bottom nav. Keep emergency Stop exposed in the live ribbon.

### Why these scores

Lumen Path gets 5s for preview safety, catalog, recovery/provenance, phone, and accessibility: it renders the full 292, keeps unavailable states honest, and makes every live gesture deliberate. Orientation is 4 because Stop is hidden one level down. Scene/global is 4 overall because the apply boundary is excellent but the background-change action leaves a stale preview identity and plant-control feedback was inconsistent. Backend fit is 4: the product concepts align with isolated preview, fixed scenes, globals, interactions, and receiver policy, but the documentation stays at conceptual alignment rather than a concrete route/receipt map.

## 3. Room Tune (cold ambient) — 87/100

### Verdict

Room Tune is the easiest product to hand to a host or gallery attendant. `Make the room… settle` is calm, legible, and fast, and phone behavior is excellent. As an artist, I would want its appliance front door paired with a more exact scene inspector: the running UI promises fine placement and stale behavior but exposes only coarse placement and no authored stale policy.

### Adventure log

1. **Cold orientation and safe stop**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** The persistent live line said `Boreal Hush · Cozy · 42 · saved exactly`. `Stop` was clearly attached to physical output, so it was my first click.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Live line → Stop → `Stopped, wall dark, power off`. No wrong turn.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Full live look and saved state were readable without opening an inspector. I believed the wall would change on Stop. The power-off consequence was stated immediately afterward; it would be safer to say before the click that Stop also powers off in this concept.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the always-visible live line. Put `stops output and powers off` in the button's adjacent copy before action.

2. **Find calm, audition, compare, perform**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** The already-selected phrase `Make the room… settle` matched the task exactly.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Tune → settle → browse `78 matches` → select `Boreal Hush` → Compare with `Polar Night` → choose `Set the live wall to…` → confirmation.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The ledger showed complete look/source/provider/role/readiness, and the two synchronized exact-ratio previews said neither was live. I believed the wall would change on the confirmation's final set-live action.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve sentence-shaped intent and action labels. Add a third compare slot for close finalists without reducing preview height.

3. **Receiver-native provenance and unavailable content**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I expected native content to carry both provider and preview truth in the selected-look detail.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Full browse → select receiver-native `Solar Wind Curtains` → inspect state.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The preview said `Host-build simulation preview — not receiver framebuffer readback`; provider remained receiver-native. Its action was disabled as `Build-only · preview available`. No physical change was possible.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the disabled action that explains what remains possible. State the missing build/deploy step.

4. **Compose, provoke drift, validate, save only, perform**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Scene` and its two transparent sheets suggested a bounded background-plus-clock composition.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Scene → background `Boreal Hush` + clock → change clock → `Unsaved arrangement` → `Simulate remote change` → `Wall changed elsewhere` → try Apply (still looked enabled) → blocked with `Reload live state first` → fail to find a Reload action → `Check scene` → `Save layout only` → save clears drift → Apply → review live/current and proposed → confirm.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Preview/live and globals exclusions were clear, validation checked one background and optional overlay, and save did not perform. The wrong turn was recovery: drift instructed me to reload but provided no visible reload control; saving appeared to clear drift. The running controls provided only Top/Middle/Bottom placement and no editable stale policy despite copy promising fine placement and stale behavior. I believed the wall would change only on the final Apply confirmation.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Partial, 3/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the two-sheet metaphor and state language. Put a real `Reload observed wall` action in the drift notice, disable Apply while drifted, and expose integer translation plus stale policy and known-fallback selection.

5. **Change Vibe and plant material independently**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Room + plants` sounded global rather than part of the current look.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Room + plants → `Vivid` → `Illuminate` strength `50` → choose field/surface options → Apply → review modal saying scene unchanged → confirm.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Global character, plant semantics, brightness/FPS/operator pace, and authored scene were separated. Exclusive field/surface choices were structurally clear. I believed globals would change only on confirmation.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the plain-language resolved plant sentence. Keep advanced output tuning folded by default.

6. **Expected-degraded receiver diagnosis**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Wall care` promised a physical outcome, so I looked there before technical metrics.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Wall care → `The picture is reaching all four wall sections` → inspect receivers 3/4 → unfold technical evidence.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The concept said write-only return paths were expected-degraded and that zero receiver errors cannot prove LED wiring. It was honest, though less precise about which playback agreement is actually observed than Wall Conductor or Lumen Path. No physical change was implied.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 4/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the physical wall-section diagram and observability caveat. Name receipt/cadence evidence separately from camera-visible appearance.

7. **Rehearse point and hole input**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Play` looked like the direct-interaction area.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Play → choose `Make hole` → tap exact wall → status `x16, y69 local only` → inspect Point and D-pad.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The surface clearly said the prototype was local and never contacted hardware. I therefore did **not** believe the physical wall would change; a production version needs an explicit live-target transition.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success as rehearsal, 5/5; partial as live control.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the honest local-only declaration. Add a separate, receipt-bearing `Send this gesture live` step.

8. **Phone authoring at ~390 px**
   - **Starting assumption / first tap:** The live card and horizontal intention rail preserved `settle` as the first decision.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Tune → settle → scan complete wrapped names → exact tall preview → compare/set-live. Bottom navigation exposed Tune, Scene, Room + plants, and Wall care; Play moved out of the primary posture.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Identity, isolated preview labeling, and final live confirmation all survived the reflow. No wrong turn.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the appliance decision order. Keep Play reachable from the intent rail or More with a visible label so it is not effectively lost on phone.

### Why these scores

Room Tune earns 5s for orientation, preview safety, and phone; its front door is immediate and its comparison is credible. Catalog is 4 because full browse reports `52 sources / 292 presets` but the running result count changes to `300 matches`, and only a subset is initially rendered behind `Show 12 more`. Scene/global and recovery are 4 overall because the global model is excellent while scene recovery and authoring controls are incomplete. Backend fit is 3: the concepts align, but candidate docs lack a concrete API/receipt map and the running scene surface does not demonstrate the contract's full translation/stale/fallback controls. Accessibility is 4 for wrapped names, focusable controls, and good reflow, with progressive results and the absent phone Play destination as remaining friction.

## 4. Wall Studio (Finder) — 68/100

### Verdict

Wall Studio makes expert information easy to inspect. The source list, columns, contextual inspector, and operational evidence feel appropriate for a commissioning laptop. Its live strip and scene save/apply separation are strong. It falls sharply because the running product contradicts its own safe-state claim: a build-only developer item presents an enabled `Take Live…` and opens a normal performance confirmation. A polished confirmation cannot repair an invalid action entering the confirmation path.

### Adventure log

1. **Cold orientation and safe stop**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** The graphite strip said `ON AIR · Cozy Ember Canopy — Hearthside · host.python / background`, Saved, FPS, power, and brightness. `Stop Output` looked unambiguously physical.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Live strip → Stop Output → `Output stopped, power remains on`.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Full component/preset/provider/role and output state were persistent. I believed the wall would change on the Stop click.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve the dense live strip and distinction between Stop and power-off. Use provider spelling consistent with the backend (`python`, `receiver_native`) rather than UI-specific variants unless a legend explains them.

2. **Find calm, audition, compare, perform**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** Finder search looked like the fastest route through hundreds, so I typed `quiet` and pressed Enter.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Search `quiet` → **no filtering or visible state change** → recover manually through All Content → Ambient Art → component → preset → `Add to Compare` → unexpectedly switches immediately to Compare View and auto-seeds Hearthside, Dusk Study, and Quiet Passage → `Take This Live…` on Quiet Passage → confirm.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Once reached, the three exact tall previews carried complete names, component, provider, role, and readiness. The auto-filled set was useful but violated the meaning of `Add`. The final sheet clearly named what would and would not change. I believed the wall would change on the sheet's final Take Live.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Partial, 3/5.** Search failure is serious at this scale, although manual drill-down and comparison worked.
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve hierarchical columns and the three-up comparison. Make search actually filter (including clear/no-results feedback), and label an auto-seeded action `Compare with related looks`.

3. **Receiver-native provenance and unavailable content**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** I expected Developer/disabled items to stay inspectable but lack performance actions, as the source rows suggested.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Developer → `Build-only & Quarantined` button (inert) → recover through All Content → Developer & Test → select disabled `Native Overlay Lease Torture Test — Hearthside` marked Build-only → click enabled `Take Live…` → normal confirmation opens with no build-only warning → Cancel.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** Provider and readiness were legible, and ordinary receiver-native previews correctly said host-build simulation, not framebuffer readback. But this build-only item's inspector also exposed enabled Add-to-Compare and Take-Live actions. I could not trust availability. The dialog made me believe the wall would change if I confirmed, despite the item's stated inability to run.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Failure, 1/5.** I cancelled rather than testing an internally contradictory action.
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve visible disabled inventory. Derive every consequential action from one authoritative compatibility/readiness gate; never allow a generic modal to be opened for build-only, unavailable, or quarantined content.

4. **Compose, dirty state, validate, save only, perform**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Scene Composer` in the source list looked like the expected fixed scene editor.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Scene Composer → select outline Background/Clock/Validation → attempt background change in dropdown (tree identity does not update) → set clock opacity `82 → 65`, translation `8` → Validate → Save Layout → confirmation names exclusions → save new revision → observe UI still labeled `Modified` → live strip unchanged. The take-live sheet remained separate.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The exact tall whole-scene preview said isolated host simulation and represented a receiver-native background using a known Python fallback. Placement, clipping, stale policy, and exclusions were clear. The wrong turns were state bugs: selection did not propagate to the tree and a successful save did not clear `Modified`. I believed the wall would change only on a separate scene-performance confirmation, not on Save.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Partial, 3/5.**
   - **Preserve / fix:** Preserve outline nodes plus contextual inspector and explicit save sheet. Make selected identity, tree, preview, validation revision, save receipt, and dirty badge one atomic state projection.

5. **Change Vibe and plant material independently**
   - **Starting assumption / first click:** `Vibe & Plant Material` was a dedicated source-list destination, correctly outside Scene Composer.
   - **Path and wrong turns:** Vibe & Plant Material → Vivid → Illuminate `50` → exclusive Field/Surface menus → `Apply Global Settings…` → confirmation says presets/scenes unchanged → apply.
   - **Legibility and consequence:** The pane explicitly said globals are never saved in content/scenes. It enforced exclusive plant groups structurally. I believed globals would change on the sheet's final Apply.
   - **Result / confidence:** **Success, 5/5.**

## Cross-candidate kit of parts

These patterns should survive regardless of which paradigm wins:

1. **Persistent physical truth:** Use Wall Conductor's or Finder's live strip: full provider-qualified live identity, saved/draft/drift relation, power, brightness, and immediate Stop. Never let the work surface replace it.
2. **Purpose before taxonomy:** Start with Lumen Path's human purpose or Room Tune's `Make the room…` phrase, then allow experts to unfold the catalog and Scene Studio.
3. **A textual scale surface:** Use Wall Conductor's complete-name reading index or Lumen Path's field guide. Do not represent 292 identities as unlabeled hairlines, truncated cards, or hover-only text.
4. **One large exact preview, then three-up compare:** Preserve the 32:138 aspect everywhere. Use Wall Conductor's audition stage and Lumen Loom's poster-scale Light table, with synchronized time and complete identity/provenance captions.
5. **Three facts, never one badge:** Show provider, preview provenance, and runtime availability separately. `receiver_native`, `host simulation / no framebuffer readback`, and `build-only` answer different questions.
6. **One authoritative availability gate:** Preview-only content can remain visible and comparable, but every drag target, Place, Take Live, keyboard command, and modal entry must derive from the same readiness/compatibility policy and fail closed.
7. **A strict state lattice:** Always name physical Live, isolated Preview/Draft, Saved layout, and observed Drift separately. Bind labels to revisions/receipts, not optimistic UI events.
8. **A fixed scene score:** One background plus optional clock overlay; provider-qualified identity; exact opacity and integer strip/LED translation; clipping; stale hold/hide/fallback; mandatory known Python fallback; validation against the current revision.
9. **Three scene verbs:** `Validate`, `Save layout only`, and `Take live` must remain separate. Save must exclude Vibe, plants, brightness, FPS, and operator speed. Take-live should compare old and proposed state and either confirm or require a short continuous hold.
10. **Independent global atmosphere:** Give Vibe and plant material their own resolved sentence, compatibility evidence, dirty state, and Apply receipt. Do not bundle global changes into scene persistence or require replaying the scene.
11. **Gesture staging proportional to consequence:** Immediate direct input is acceptable in an explicitly live play mode; elsewhere use Lumen Path's rehearsal → exact-coordinate receipt → Confirm send sequence.
12. **Policy-driven health:** Begin with “can the show continue?”, then disclose frame agreement, telemetry completeness, acknowledgement, release acceptance, freshness, and the installed expected-degraded policy. Never infer LED health merely from zero receiver errors.
13. **Real responsive hierarchy:** Copy Lumen Path's contextual phone navigation and Room Tune's decision order. Keep live truth and Stop pinned, make compare a sheet rather than an obstruction, remove inactive desktop panes from the accessibility tree, and never shrink the desktop canvas.
14. **Atomic identity projection:** A selection change must update component/preset/provider, preview image and heading, validation revision, comparison receipt, save state, and take-live receipt together. Lumen Path's stale heading and Finder's stale tree/dirty badge are exactly the class of failure this prevents.

## Final acceptance bar

Before selecting a visual direction for implementation, I would run the same adventures against one integrated slice and require: complete 52/292 identity at desktop and phone, zero enabled performance actions for non-runnable content, preview headings that cannot diverge from staged receipts, native-background validation that fails without a known Python fallback, save-only that demonstrably leaves live state unchanged, globals that apply independently, and expected-degraded receiver copy sourced from actual policy and fresh evidence. Those are show-safety requirements, not polish.
